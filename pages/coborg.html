Backpack-Mounted Third Robot Arm

Motion planning and stabilization for COBORG, a wearable robotic arm that assists technicians with overhead panel installation.

84.62%task success in panel installation tests

COBORG is a backpack-mounted exoskeleton designed to act as an extra hand for overhead manufacturing and assembly. The platform helps an operator support and position panels while reducing the need for a second person.

My contribution

  • Led the motion planning subsystem in C++ for a 4-DOF wearable robotic arm.
  • Developed a multi-stage pipeline transitioning from end-effector position control to force control using MoveIt and Octomap in ROS Melodic.
  • Implemented a resolved rate controller to compensate for user motion of up to 6 inches of translation and 90 degrees of rotation.

Results

The system achieved an 84.62% task success rate and a 100% safety success rate in panel installation tests with panels weighing up to 15 lbs.
